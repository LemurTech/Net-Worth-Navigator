The Setup Panel at `http://localhost:8010/setup` is the primary way to create, edit, and manage your scenarios. It has six tabs and a global action bar. Timeline decisions such as retirement, Social Security claiming, and the modeled end of plan live in **Events**, not in the person profile.

## Quick Start

- Start the editor: `.venv/bin/python admin_app.py` (Linux/macOS) or `.venv\Scripts\python.exe admin_app.py` (Windows)
- Open **[http://localhost:8010/setup](http://localhost:8010/setup)**
- Click **New from Template**
- Choose **Single-Person** or **Couple / Two-Person**, enter a name, and click **Create**
- You're now in the Setup Panel for your new scenario. Work through the tabs below; the first five cover the normal workflow, while Raw TOML remains available for advanced inspection or bulk edits.
- Click **Save** to save your changes
- Click **Validate** to check for configuration errors
- Click **Re-render** to generate the projection

**Tip:** The **Withdrawal / Surplus Order** settings in the Metadata tab control the priority of account buckets. Feel free to leave the defaults — they're sensible for most households.

## The Six Tabs

### Metadata Tab

This tab holds your scenario's high-level settings:

- **Plan name, description, and household type** — identify the scenario and whether it is single-person or couple
- **People** — names, dates of birth, take-home income, and current employment assumptions
- **Cash targets and assumptions** — reserve targets, market assumptions, plan years, display basis, and state-tax selection
- **Advanced disclosures** — withdrawal/surplus order, spending and tax settings, RMD controls, and simulation/Monte Carlo settings

### Social Security Tab

- Enter each person's age-to-monthly-benefit table from their SSA estimate.
- Add the actual claim timing as a **SocialSecurity** event on the Events tab. The editor asks for an age and saves the corresponding year from the person's date of birth.
- NWN resolves the benefit from the table at render time; it does not store a monthly benefit on the event.

### Income & Contributions Tab

- **Employment income** — take-home pay, real raises, and whether contributions are already excluded from take-home pay
- **Contributions** — flat-dollar or percent-of-gross inputs, annual increases, employer matching, 401(k)/IRA routing, and traditional/Roth splits
- **Ownership** — RMD and Roth ownership shares for pooled balances

### Accounts Tab

Changes based on your selected data source:

- **Manual Entry:** Structured form for investable balances (taxable, traditional IRA, Roth, cash), non-investable assets, named properties, and named liabilities
- **Monarch Money:** Account table with category, owner, disabled, and basis controls plus **Refresh from Monarch**
- **CSV Import:** File upload, parsed account preview, category/owner assignment, and import

### Events Tab

Create, edit, delete, sort, or disable the event-driven timeline. Use it for **Retire**, **SocialSecurity**, and **EndOfPlan** as well as income, expenses, property changes, career events, spending changes, and contribution changes. See [Events & the Event System](/Net-Worth-Navigator/key-concepts/events/) for the modeling rules.

### Raw TOML Tab

A text editor for the complete scenario file. Every structured control is stored here. Use it for bulk edits or advanced configuration; after hand editing, choose **Validate** before rendering.

## Editing a Sample

Bundled sample scenarios are deliberately read-only. You can render and inspect them, but must use **Clone** or **New from Template** before saving changes.

## Actions

## Global Action Bar

At the bottom of the panel:

| Button | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Saves current changes |
| **Validate** | Runs semantic validation on your config |
| **Save + Re-render** | Saves and renders the current scenario |
| **Save + Render All** | Saves and re-renders every scenario |
| **New from Template** | Creates a new scenario from a starter template |

## Scenario Management

- **Scenario dropdown** at the top — switch between your plans. User scenarios appear first, then a separator, then sample scenarios
- **Rename** — change the slug via the edit button next to the slug
- **Default checkbox** — mark a scenario as the default (appears first in the projection shell)
- **Clone** — duplicate the current scenario (changes to suggested slug if the name exists)
- **Delete** — remove the scenario with confirmation
